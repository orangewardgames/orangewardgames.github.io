Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

WordFlock: Word Sort Solitaire (the "App") is developed and published under the developer name Orangeward Games. In this Privacy Policy, "we", "us", and "our" refer to the developer of the App. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the App, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

You do not need to create an account to play WordFlock: Word Sort Solitaire.

The App does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, payment card information, or other directly identifying information.

However, the App uses third-party services and SDKs that may automatically collect or process certain information.

Usage and Analytics Data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve gameplay, performance, features, and content.

Firebase Analytics collection is enabled in the App and may automatically collect information such as:

  • App interactions and analytics events
  • Levels played and gameplay activity
  • Session information
  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Approximate geographic information
  • Device, app, or installation identifiers
  • Install-referrer information from Google Play for installation attribution

Install-referrer information may be used to determine how an installation was attributed, such as the source or campaign associated with the installation.

We also use Firebase Remote Config to remotely configure certain App settings and features. Firebase may process installation identifiers and device or App information necessary to provide this service.

Crash and Diagnostic Data

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may automatically collect information such as:

  • Crash reports and stack traces
  • App state at the time of a crash
  • Device and operating-system information
  • App version
  • Diagnostic information
  • Installation identifiers associated with Crashlytics

Advertising Data

The App displays advertisements through Appodeal, an advertising mediation platform.

Appodeal may route advertising requests to third-party advertising networks and other advertising demand sources configured or made available through its mediation platform. Advertising and attribution SDKs integrated into the App may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted
  • Install-referrer information
  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Operating-system and App information
  • Ad impressions and interactions
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Diagnostic, security, and fraud-prevention information

This information may be used to:

  • Serve advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Attribute installations or advertising conversions
  • Prevent fraud and invalid advertising activity
  • Personalize advertising where legally permitted and where the required consent has been obtained

The specific advertising networks used may change over time and may vary depending on region, device, consent status, and mediation configuration.

Purchase Data

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

We do not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Google Play processes the payment and provides the App with information necessary to confirm whether a purchase was successfully completed.

Game Progress and Settings

Game progress, coins, preferences, and other gameplay settings are stored locally on your device.

If the App is uninstalled or its local storage is cleared, this information may also be deleted.
